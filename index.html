Cashflow you can count on

Count On It shows when money actually leaves and arrives — so you see the tight weeks coming before they hit.

Budget to plan; cashflow to verify. A budget sets your intentions; cashflow checks them against reality — every bill, paycheck and debt payment on the calendar. That is the way your bank balance actually behaves, not an average of it.

Open it in your browser

The app lives at countonitapp.github.io/countonit — open that in any browser on any device. Nothing to install from an app store.

Save it as an app on your phone

iPhone (Safari): Open the page in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow up), then choose Add to Home Screen. It launches full-screen from its own icon, just like a store app.
Android (Chrome): Open the page in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu (top right), then choose Add to Home screen → Add.
Mac / PC: Just bookmark the page — or pin the tab so it's one click away.
